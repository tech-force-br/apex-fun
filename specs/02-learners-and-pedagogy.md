# 02 — Learners and pedagogy

## Who it is for

1. People who already use Salesforce every day and have never written code.
2. Salesforce admins who are afraid of developer work.

Explanations on the website stay simple. Early variable theory may use a few metaphors. Prefer plain words over cute stories.

## How teaching works

- Theory is short.
- Most time is exercises that get harder very slowly.
- Students write real Apex. Not multiple choice.
- One idea at a time. A card is either theory or one exercise, never both.
- Progress is gated. They cannot skip ahead inside a topic or to the next topic.

## Practice rules

- The editor starts empty. They type the full snippet.
- They cannot copy or paste in theory samples, prompts, or the student editor. Admin screens may paste.
- After two failed Runs on an exercise, suggest opening the Theory pane. There is no hint button and no per-card hint text in v1.
- Failed Runs show the Apex error plus a short plain-language explanation.
- They may Retry a passed exercise for practice. A failed retry does not un-pass it.

## Language of code

Apex keywords and student code stay English even when the UI is Portuguese.
