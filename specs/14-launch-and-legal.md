# 14 — Launch and legal

This is not legal advice. It is the project checklist.

## Before public students

1. Written OK or a paid license from **October Swimmer** for aer on a multi-user site.
2. Talk to a **trademark lawyer** about the name **ApexFun**.
3. Footer disclaimer live on every public page.
4. No official Salesforce mascots or SLDS unless Salesforce gives written permission.
5. Move off “personal / non-commercial” hosting plans if the site is a real product.

## What we believe is ordinary

Saying “this site teaches Salesforce Apex,” showing Apex keywords, and running student snippets, with a clear **not affiliated** line.

## What is higher risk

- Product name and domain that include **Apex** while teaching that language
- Official characters, logos, or Trailhead screenshots

Other learning brands already use Apex in the name (Learn Apex, Apex Hours). That does not grant permission.

## Name decision

Keep **ApexFun** while building. Recheck before launch. Change the public name if a lawyer says to.
